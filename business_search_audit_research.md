# Indian Commercial Solar Lead Quality — Research Notes

## Evidence Relevant to Sales Squad

Commercial and industrial (C&I) rooftop solar is not a generic small-business product. The strongest buying conditions are sustained electricity use, a viable site, decision-maker access, and a state/distribution-company environment that permits a workable project. JMK Research and IEEFA identify rising C&I grid tariffs and growing electricity demand as key adoption drivers; their report also states that C&I rooftop solar historically represents most of India’s rooftop installations. [1]

The current target geography is strategically sensible: IEEFA/JMK identify **Telangana and Andhra Pradesh** among the more favourable rooftop-solar ecosystems, while noting that regulatory rules, net-metering approval, and financing affect feasibility. [2] This means the product should not treat business type alone as proof of a viable solar opportunity.

## Implications for Lead Search

1. Prioritize **electricity-intense operations** first: manufacturers, cold chains, food processing, textile mills, hospitals, large hotels, warehouses with refrigeration, and multi-shift facilities.
2. Treat a Google rating, review count, or generic phone number as only a weak proxy for buying ability. They measure discoverability more than energy consumption, roof control, or authority to buy.
3. Add separate evidence for: facility ownership or long lease, day-load pattern, estimated bill band, roof/parking potential, business scale/multi-location, decision-maker identity, and contact confidence.
4. Build a regulatory feasibility check by state/DISCOM rather than using a blanket subsidy or savings claim.
