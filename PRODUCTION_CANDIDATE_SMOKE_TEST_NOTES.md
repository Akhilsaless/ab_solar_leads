# Production Candidate Chatbot Smoke-Test Notes

## Controlled Test Session

- Runtime under test: current unpublished candidate preview at `/chat/sales-squad`.
- No publishing, outreach, email, OTP, WhatsApp, or customer-contact action was triggered.
- Residential prompt submitted: `Hyderabad, residential, ₹6500 monthly electricity bill`.
- Observation as of 2026-09-09: the prompt appeared in the chat and completed after approximately 27.6 seconds. Klaus recognized residential use and ₹6,500, but re-asked for the state despite Hyderabad being supplied. The initial response did not present an estimate; it requested state selection. After selecting Telangana, the follow-up remained in `Klaus is thinking…` during the subsequent 12-second observation window. No contact data was submitted and no lead-capture action was requested.
- Final residential-flow result: after an extended wait, the state-selection turn returned `I apologize, I'm having trouble responding right now. Please try again.` The network log did not record a successful completed request for that turn. This blocks completion of the estimate journey and is a release-blocking chatbot reliability failure.
- The browser-local key `smart-chat-session-sales-squad` was cleared after the residential test so subsequent tests can begin as separate internal sessions. Existing server-side test conversation data was left unchanged.
- Commercial prompt submitted in a separate browser-local session: `I have a warehouse in Hyderabad and my monthly electricity bill is ₹85000`. At submission, the interface displayed the prompt and `Klaus is thinking…`; no contact information, consent, or handoff action was provided.
- Commercial response completed after approximately 54.5 seconds. It calculated a preliminary approximate 71 kW commercial estimate, explicitly showed `Govt Subsidy −₹0`, used conditional savings language, and proposed a site visit rather than restarting the opening flow. The response then exposed an additional generic bill-choice/upload panel after the estimate, which is unnecessary clutter in an already qualified conversation.
- The persistent `Talk to a person` button opened a consent-gated handoff panel at the current conversation position. It stated that only the remaining contact detail would be requested and provided no OTP or claim that a call/message had already been sent. Consent was not selected and no continue action or contact data was submitted.
- Price objection submitted in the same commercial context: `Solar is too expensive for me`. The interface retained the existing estimate and displayed `Klaus is thinking…`; no contact data or communication action was submitted.
- The price-objection response completed after approximately 20.9 seconds. It directly acknowledged the concern, retained the commercial context, stated that residential subsidy does not apply to commercial installs, and offered conditional financing, phased-install, and accountant-reviewed tax options. It did not restart the generic pitch. The browser-local session was then cleared to isolate the subsidy-question test.
- Subsidy-question prompt submitted in a fresh session: `How much government subsidy can I get for solar in Hyderabad?`. At submission, the interface displayed the message and `Klaus is thinking…`; no contact, consent, or customer communication action was submitted.
- The subsidy response completed after approximately 25.6 seconds. It did not give a universal amount, framed assistance as residential rooftop support subject to household category, system size, ownership, and eligibility rules, and asked only whether the request concerned a home or commercial property. A government guidance link was displayed. The browser-local session was then cleared to isolate the human-handoff test.
- Human-handoff prompt submitted in a fresh session: `Call me tomorrow`. At submission, the interface displayed the prompt and `Klaus is thinking…`; no contact information or consent was provided, and no outbound communication was triggered.
- The human-handoff response completed after approximately 17.0 seconds. It stated that a call could occur tomorrow and requested the two missing inputs—time and phone number—without displaying OTP or claiming delivery. However, the natural-language path did not surface an explicit consent request at this point; consent is confirmed only through the persistent handoff panel. The browser-local session was cleared without providing any contact information.
- Returning-session setup: a fresh browser-local session received the non-contact residential prompt `Hyderabad, residential, ₹6500 monthly electricity bill`. The response was pending at the point of setup; the browser session key was intentionally retained for a subsequent reload check.
- Returning-session result: after a page reload with the retained browser-local key, the prior message and state request were restored. However, a new `Namaste! ... May I know your name?` welcome appeared above the prior context, and the assistant’s `[QUICK_REPLIES]` tag was rendered as literal text rather than usable reply buttons. Context retention therefore works, but the reload behavior unnecessarily reintroduces a greeting and exposes raw formatting.
- Bill-upload result: no safe internal electricity-bill image or PDF fixture was available in the project, so no upload/OCR request was made and no customer or lead data was created. This journey remains unverified.
- Mobile initial-screen result at 375 × 812: the header, greeting, three quick replies, input field, preliminary-estimate disclaimer, and the compact Talk-to-a-person entry point were visible without overlap. The full-page capture initially rendered blank before initialization, then the viewport capture rendered correctly after a short wait. A mobile estimate-card state was not exercised because doing so would require another long-running chatbot turn and an internal bill/contact-free flow was already found to be unreliable.
- Safety and side-effect verification: `outreachConfig.agencyId=1` and `automatedOutreachConfig.agencyId=1` both remained `isEnabled=0`. In the smoke-test time window, six anonymous, no-contact conversation records were created; none contained a phone or email and none were marked as customer or company email sent. No leads, outreach runs, outreach records, or inbound email-event logs were created in the test window. No OTP or automated WhatsApp interaction appeared in any exercised flow.
